# Review 2026-09-25 — eight layout blocks

Two independent reviewers read the eight module boards from the files only.
They did not see the layout sessions. Each finding has an ID, a severity and a
disposition. "Measured" means a number from pcbnew geometry or kicad-cli.

Reviewer R1: Ethernet-W5500-Magjack, RF-CC1101-433, MCU-ESP32S3-MINI.
Reviewer R2: PowerMux-TPS2121, Buck-5V-3V3-TPS563208, LDO-RF-3V3-AP2112K,
USBC-Device-ESD, LED-Status-x3.

All eight boards: DRC 0 errors, 0 unconnected, 0 parity. Every canary fires.
Every interface net reaches its breakout via.

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Board | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1-1, R2-1 | blocker | Ethernet, RF, PowerMux, PoE module | The "Fine-pitch fanout clearance" rule matches every zone that overlaps the IC courtyard. The 0.10 mm clearance then applies to the whole pour. RF pour sat 0.10 mm from `/ANT_433` (class 0.25 mm) | **Fixed** on RF and PowerMux: zones excluded, DRC unchanged. **Open** on Ethernet and the PoE module: their clean DRC depends on the leak (4 and 5 starved thermals without it) |
| R1-2 | blocker | Ethernet, PoE module | Chip-side copper sits 0.26 mm from line-side J2 pad 7 (VC1P), and LED_LINK_K sits 0.51 mm from J2 pads 1 and 7. The jack's own pad field is 1.04 mm or more. The J2 courtyard exception hides it | **Open.** Reroute the MDI_RDP escape and LED_LINK_K. Remove the courtyard exception |
| R1-3 | should-fix | Ethernet | 8 GND vias, 4 of them in the breakout row. Y1, C61, C62 GND pads are 4.7 to 7.8 mm from a via | **Open.** Also closes the starved thermals of R1-1 |

## What the reviews confirmed

- Every `.kicad_dru` exception other than the fanout rule hides only its own
  items: J3 edge, J1 and switch pegs, U3 hole-to-hole.
- RF chain order matches SWRR046. C20 is 12 pF and C21 is 15 pF.
- MCU antenna is fully off the board, HDG position 1, 2.0 mm feed-side strip.
- R70 is 169 k in the schematic and on the board.
